Bullying In South Auckland needs to stop

Everyone deserves to feel safe, respected and supported.

What is Bullying?

Bullying is when someone repeatedly hurts, threatens, or may leave another person on purpose. Bullying can be described as many factors such as physical, verbal, social, or online.

Through South Auckland, bullying may occur in schools, in neighbourhoods, or even on social internet. One example can be of a person who is bullied because of thier culture, appearence, family, or the way they pronounce words. Bullying can make people feel emotions like sadness, lonely, scared, or unsafe. Its very important that students and others to speak up through people or websites that might offer help.

What are the effects of bullying?

Bullying can have serious, long-lasting effects, causing fear, anxiety, depression, low self-esteem, and even suicidal thoughts. It can harm school performance, relationships, and future opportunities.

Talking to someone you trust can make a big difference. This could be a parent, teacher, school counsellor, family member or another trusted adult.

Getting Support

Nobody should have to deal with bullying alone. Reaching out for support is an important step.

If you see someone being bullied,you can support them by listening, including them and telling a trusted adult when help is needed.
