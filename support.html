What if I am scared to tell someone?

If you or someome else you know is scared to tell someone about being bullied, just remember that you aren't alone and that its not your fault. It maybe difficult to express the burden of being bullied because you might feel embarrassed, worried, or afraid because of how much more the bullying will increase. Example can be you can start by talking to someone you can trust, such as a parent or others. If speaking one to one is hard for you , you can write down what happening through your life at school or anywhere else or can send messages to them. You can also contact Youthline for confidential support.
